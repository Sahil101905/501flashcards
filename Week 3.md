BMES 501 Wk03: HW IA2-PS1, P.5: Living systems are highly ordered. Explain briefly how life is compatible with the 2nd law of thermodynamics.
??
Organisms are not closed systems: they exchange matter and energy with their environment.<br>They take in ordered food and release less-ordered waste and heat, so the disorder added to the surroundings exceeds the order created; total entropy of the universe still increases.
